01 / Biomedical signalsMay 2026 — Present

Understanding movement through IMU sensor data.

Volunteer Researcher · Neuromodulation Center, Department of Neurology
PI: Prof. Biswaranjan Mohanty

I work with six-axis inertial sensor recordings to study movement and gait patterns relevant to Parkinson’s disease. Much of the work starts before modeling: inspecting raw signals, cleaning and segmenting recordings, and building consistent features.

I’ve trained and compared seven classifiers, including Random Forest and Extra Trees. The initial reported Macro-F1 is 0.60; the next part of the work looks at whether gait-specific features help.

Methods and current work
  • Signal cleaning, transformation, resampling, segmentation, and feature engineering.
  • Time- and frequency-domain exploration, including power spectral density and spectrogram analysis.
  • Model comparisons and hyperparameter tuning; gait-feature analysis is ongoing.

02 / Electronic health recordsMay 2026 — Present

Looking for patient subtypes in ICU records.

Capstone Researcher · Department of Surgery, Division of Computational Health Sciences
PI: Prof. Jocelyn Chi

ICU records hold repeated measurements, missing values, and differences in how patients are documented. I build a patient-level dataset from the first 24 hours of care and use unsupervised learning to examine possible patient groups.

I compare K-Means with alternative clustering approaches and assess the solutions using clustering metrics, stability, and clinical interpretation. The goal is to examine whether the resulting groups differ meaningfully in severity and outcomes.

Methods and current work
  • Cohort construction, data-quality checks, aggregation of repeat measurements, missing-data handling, and normalization.
  • Within-cluster sum of squares, silhouette score, cluster sizes, stability, and interpretability.
  • Clinical characterization using illness severity, mortality, length of stay, and patient profiles.

PROJECT / 001 — CLINICAL NLP
May 2026 · Clinical NLP / deep learning

Temporal Clinical Reasoning for Medical Coding

Can the sequence of clinical notes help predict the diagnosis and procedure codes for a hospital admission? I built patient-level data from MIMIC-III, ordered clinical notes by time, and compared BioClinicalBERT, Longformer, and a BiLSTM-based temporal model.

The models were evaluated with Micro-F1, Macro-F1, and Precision@5. The temporal model reached 0.6096 CPT Micro-F1 and 0.3971 CPT Macro-F1.

PROJECT / 002 — FORECASTING
December 2025 · Data analytics / time series

COVID-19 Forecasting & Analytics Web Application

I built a Streamlit app to explore COVID-19 data by country, compare trends, and generate forecasts. The application cleans and aggregates public-health time series, then presents the results through Plotly charts, maps, and downloadable views.

Forecasts use Prophet with weekly and yearly seasonality and 95% prediction intervals.
